1. Parts

  • Graph tab: pods as groups, seats as nodes, edges from /api/rigs/:rigId (the server reads it anyway; the graph route's positions are all zero, so RigApp lays it out), live status colours; click a seat to open its thread.
  • Terminal tab: the seat's tmux pane through xterm.js over the daemon's terminal socket, read-only by default. Falls back to a 3 s preview when the socket is off.
  • Take control: a toggle with the risk notice before your keys reach the pane.
  • From Operant: its xterm theme and key mapping setup, not its local shells.
  • Tests: Playwright for open the graph, open a seat from it, attach the terminal.

2. Questions for doole

  1. Approve @xyflow/react (graph), @xterm/xterm and @xterm/addon-fit? Claude recommends: yes; MIT, and OpenRig's own UI uses the same two.

3. How to test each step

Step: Graph

  1. Run & "E:\REPOS\AI TOOLS\RigApp\tools\dev.ps1" and open http://localhost:7481.
  2. Open lead under kernel and click Graph at the top: Advisor on top, Operator and Queue below, 4 seats, with delegates, escalates and collaborates links. The lead has a teal outline.
  3. Click queue.worker in the graph: its chat opens.
  4. The dots change colour as seats work; the legend at the bottom left says which colour is which. The + and − buttons zoom.

Step: Terminal and Take control

  1. Open lead and click Terminal: the seat's screen shows. With the daemon's terminal socket off (it is now), the top line says it is a copy refreshed every 3 seconds.
  2. For live output instead, in Ubuntu run rig config set ui.enabled true, then rig daemon stop and rig daemon start. RigApp then says "Live". Seats keep running through the restart.
  3. Click Take control: a warning asks first. Take control puts an amber outline round the terminal and your keys go into the seat. Give back control makes it read-only again.
  4. Tests: pnpm test:e2e includes the graph and terminal checks.

4. Review checklist (about 30 minutes)

  • The kernel graph matches its spec: 3 pods, 4 seats, 3 cross-pod edges.
  • Statuses change colour live.
  • The terminal shows the seat's screen and stays read-only until you take control.
